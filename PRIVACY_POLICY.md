# Privacy Policy for Vivichi

**Last updated:** September 12, 2026

Vivichi ("the app") is developed by Syphen. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your information.

## Data Collection

Vivichi does **not** collect, transmit, or share any personal data with the developer or any third party. The app does not have a server, does not require an account or login, and does not use analytics or advertising SDKs.

## Data Storage

All information you enter in Vivichi — your pet's name and species, habit names and schedules, streaks, XP, achievement progress, and settings — is stored **locally on your device only**, using Android's private app storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer.

If you uninstall the app, this data is permanently deleted from your device.

## Notifications

Vivichi uses Android's local notification and alarm system to remind you about your habits. These reminders are scheduled and triggered entirely on your device — no data about your habits or usage is sent anywhere to make this work.

## Third-Party Services

Vivichi does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Permissions

The app requests the following device permissions, used solely for their stated purpose:
- **Notifications** — to show habit reminders.
- **Alarms & reminders (exact alarms)** — to schedule habit reminders at the correct time.
- **Boot completed** — to re-enable your scheduled reminders after your phone restarts.

None of these permissions are used to collect or transmit data.

## Sharing Feature

If you choose to use the in-app "Share Streak" feature, an image is generated on your device and handed to your phone's own share sheet (e.g. Messages, WhatsApp, Instagram) — this only happens when you explicitly tap Share, and the image is only sent to the app/contact you personally choose.

## Children's Privacy

Vivichi does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data at all.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in the future (for example, if analytics or ads are added), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
